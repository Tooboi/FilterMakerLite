## Filter Maker Lite 0.7.1

The free pleated filter generator for Blender now includes the **Boolean tool**, so you can set a filter into your model without leaving the add-on.

### New
- **Boolean tool**: cut the source mesh with a box, an envelope of the pleated form or any object. Make a filter *From Faces* on a housing, turn on Boolean (Envelope, Difference), and the housing is cut around it. The cut is applied when you click **Finish (Bake)**.
- **UVs unrolled across the folds**: textures keep their size on every flank and on the frame.
- **Apply Curve Edit**: reshape a curve source in Edit Mode, then click **Apply Curve Edit** in the panel to rebuild the filter. (Curve edits are no longer followed automatically in the background, which kept Blender doing work on every scene change.)

### Improved
- Faster rebuilds of face and curve filters.

### Install
Get it from **Edit → Preferences → Get Extensions** (search *Filter Maker Lite*), or download `FilterMakerLite-0.7.1.zip` below and drag it into Blender. Requires Blender 4.2 or newer; tested on 5.2 LTS.
